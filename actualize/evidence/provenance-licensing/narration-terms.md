# Narration rights: what the sources say (read 2026-10-03)
Model used (required by the brief, never substituted): `fish-audio/s2.1-pro-free:free` through OpenRouter, default voice, one synthesis of the whole script.
- https://fish.audio/s2/ ("What is the license for S2 Pro?"): "S2 Pro is licensed under the Fish Audio Research License. Research and non-commercial use is permitted free of charge. Commercial use requires a separate license from Fish Audio."
- https://huggingface.co/fishaudio/s2-pro/raw/main/LICENSE.md: "This Agreement is intended to allow research and non-commercial uses of the Materials free of charge. Any Commercial use of the Materials requires a separate license from Fish Audio."
- https://fish.audio/terms/: paid services are licensed for commercial use; free use is internal, personal, non-commercial.
- https://openrouter.ai/terms: "Your ownership rights in the Output are set forth in the Model Terms for each Model you use."
- OpenRouter's model description: the free variant is "intended for testing, prototyping, and low-volume applications."
Not found: the Model Terms for this specific free endpoint, and any statement that audio produced through it may be published commercially.
Consequence: the voiceover is usable for validation and internal review. Publishing a product film with it is a commercial use whose licence is unconfirmed (U14). The film's source and script do not depend on the voice, so the rights question can be closed by licensing the model, or by re-voicing with a model whose terms allow it, without touching the picture.
