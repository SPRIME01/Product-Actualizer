# Mote (product page, v0.2, February 2026)

Meet Mote, the desk companion that follows you around the room with its head.

- Hears you from across the room.
- Just 85 mm wide. Fits anywhere.
- Up to 8 hours on its built-in battery.
- Touch the top to wake it.
- Completely private: everything stays on the device.
- Built on a Raspberry Pi Zero 2 W.
- Temperature and humidity sensing, accurate to 0.3 C.
