Mote is a little desk companion robot. It sits next to your monitor, hears you, looks at what you hold
up, talks back, and follows you around the desk with its head. It's about 85 mm across so it fits
anywhere. Runs from the wall adapter in the box. Everything stays on the device, nothing in the cloud.

I want to put a closed beta kit in front of 10 builders. Price maybe $149. The name is Mote, the
voice is warm and short, never chirpy. I have a rev C board on my desk now and the firmware compiles.
The old product page is still up from February; I think it is mostly right.
