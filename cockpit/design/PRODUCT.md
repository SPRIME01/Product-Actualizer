# Cockpit: product context

register: product

## Users
The owner of a real product (software, hardware, or both) who is being taken from incomplete to launchable by an agent, and the agent itself. The owner works in long sessions, usually beside a terminal, at a desk with mixed light. They are technical, not necessarily a frontend person. They need to understand what the agent found, judge it, decide, and trust that nothing happened behind their back.

## Purpose
A shared workbench over the actualization process. It shows evidence, comparisons, contradictions, staleness, and physical preflights as the agent composes them, and it turns the owner's judgement into recorded input for the process. It is a projection of the run directory and an input channel to the inbox. It is never a second source of truth and never a second decision system.

## Tone
Instrument panel, not dashboard. Quiet, dense, legible. Familiar patterns over invention (tabs, splits, tables, command palette). Nothing decorative; color only carries process meaning (grade, status, severity).

## Anti-references
Generic SaaS admin dashboards with card grids and hero metrics. Chat applications with attachments. Anything that animates on load.

## Principles
1. The rail is the process; the workspace is the problem. The rail never grows past a fifth of the viewport.
2. Each surface offers the next useful affordance (finding to evidence to comparison to decision to consequence), not every control.
3. Authority is visible: process data is plain, agent-supplied data is marked, the owner's gestures say what they record and what they do not change.
4. Fast and keyboard friendly; state changes in 150 ms or less; reduced motion respected.
